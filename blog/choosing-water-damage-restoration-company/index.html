Selecting a Restoration Professional in Reading, PA

Home / Blog / How to Find the Right Restoration Company in Reading, PA

When water damage strikes your Reading, PA property, choosing the right restoration company is one of the most important decisions you'll make. That choice affects the speed of the restoration, how completely the damage gets fixed, what comes out of your own pocket, and whether mold or other secondary problems appear months down the road. Not every outfit is qualified, and hiring the wrong one can mean inflated invoices, a job left incomplete, or something worse. Here is what to look for when sorting the best restoration firms from everyone else.

Check for IICRC Certification

In the restoration industry, the leading credential comes from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC). To earn certification, a technician trains rigorously in restoration science, covering mold prevention, moisture measurement, structural drying, and proper extraction technique. Ask any restoration company you're considering whether their technicians hold current IICRC certifications in Water Damage Restoration (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD). If a company cannot prove those credentials, move on.

Confirm Insurance and Licensing

Workers' compensation and general liability coverage are must-haves for a restoration contractor on your property. Request certificates of insurance and verify they're current. If the contractor is underinsured, an injury or further damage during the job could become your liability. Licensing matters too, so confirm the firm holds whatever business licenses Reading or Pennsylvania requires.

Evaluate Response Time and Availability

A water loss gets worse the longer it waits. Each hour lost adds to both the damage and the bill. Real 24/7 emergency service, with crews that reach your neighborhood fast, should be part of the deal. Ask specifically about their response time to Reading and confirm that their emergency line is answered by a live person, not a voicemail or answering service that adds delay.

Find Out What Equipment and Methods They Use

Professional restoration requires professional equipment. Three questions cover it: do they use thermal imaging to detect moisture, are their dehumidifiers LGR units (the industry standard), and is extraction done with truck-mounted equipment, which outperforms portable-only operations? Ask how drying is monitored too, because a reputable company checks moisture every day and repositions equipment according to the readings instead of setting it up and walking away.

Look Into Reviews and Past Customers

A company's history shows up clearly in its online reviews. Check Google, Yelp, and the BBB for consistent patterns of quality, professionalism, and customer satisfaction. Focus on comments covering the finished quality of the work, insurance assistance, whether crews were on time, and how well the company communicated. Feel free to request references from water damage jobs the company finished recently in Reading.

Understand Pricing and Insurance Billing

Reputable restoration companies provide detailed estimates before beginning work and can explain their pricing structure clearly. Xactimate and similar industry-standard pricing programs, recognized and accepted by insurance carriers, are used by many quality contractors. Ask whether the company works directly with insurance carriers and whether they can bill your insurance directly. Companies that demand large upfront payments or refuse to work with insurance should raise concerns.
