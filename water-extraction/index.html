Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working

The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too.
